# Kundli House Inspector V1

The North Indian Kundli in the saved Birth Chart Viewer is interactive.

## Behaviour

- House 1 is selected by default.
- Clicking a house, or focusing it and pressing Enter/Space, selects it.
- The inspector shows the selected house number, engine-provided sign and lord.
- Planet cards show the engine-provided degree, nakshatra and retrograde status.
- Empty houses are explicitly identified.
- The frontend does not calculate signs, houses, lords, degrees, nakshatras or retrograde motion.

This is a presentation layer over the deterministic saved-profile chart response.
